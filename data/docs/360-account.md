## Description

Earn higher interest rates when you credit your salary, transact, insure and invest.

## Eligibility

- Minimum Age : 18 years old
- Nationality : Singaporeans, Permanent Residents, E-Pass and S-Pass Holders

## Initial deposit

S$1,000

## Minimum balance

S$3,000 ( Minimum average daily balance for the month )

## Fees and charges

S$2 (waived for the first year)

## Benefits

Bonus interest on the first S$70,000 of your account balance when you do all or any of these:
- 1.2% per year - Credit your salary of at least S$2,000 through GIRO.
- 0.3% per year - Pay at least 3 bills online or through GIRO with a total amount of at least S$150.
- 0.3% per year - Spend at least S$500 on OCBC Credit Cards.
- 1.2% per year - Insure or invest with OCBC. Earn 0.6% or 1.2% for 12 months, depending on the qualifying amount.
- 1% per year - Earn this extra bonus on the first S$70,000 if your account balance is S$200,000 and above
- You will earn a base interest of 0.05% per year on your entire account balance.
