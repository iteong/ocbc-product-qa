## Description

Earn higher interest rates for months you did not make any withdrawals.

## Eligibility

Singaporeans, Singapore PRs and foreigners age 16 and above

## Initial deposit

S$10,000

## Minimum balance

S$3,000 (Minimum average daily balance for the month)

## Fees and charges

$2 fall below fee applies

## Benefits

Interest rates:
- Keep your savings growing with up to 0.80% p.a. for months when no withdrawals are made. Withdrawals can be made any time and you will receive base interest for the months you accessed your cash.
Get up to 0.80% per year on your entire account balance:
- 0.05% per year-base interest
- 0.75% per year (15x base interest)-bonus interest for months with no withdrawals

## How to apply

- Apply at one of our branches
  - NRIC (for Singaporeans and Singapore PRs)
  - Passport(Foreigners)
  - Employment Pass (EP) or S-Pass or Student Pass(Foreigners)
  - S$10,000 (for initial deposit)
  - Include any one of the following documents:Phone bill,Half-yearly CPF statement,Any bank statement
