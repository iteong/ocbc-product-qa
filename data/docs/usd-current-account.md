## Description

A USD account with chequebook

## Eligibility

Singaporeans, Singapore PRs and foreigners age 18 and above

## Initial deposit

US$1,000 via S$ cheque,deduction from your OCBC accountor one-time transfer from another bank

## Minimum balance

US$1,000 (Minimum average daily balancefor the month)

## Fees and charges

Fall below fee-US$10

## Benefits

- Flexibility of issuing cheques in US Dollars
- Initial deposit US$1,000 via S$ cheque, deduction from your OCBC account, or one-time transfer from another bank
- This is a non-interest bearing account.
- Deposit in foreign currency notes is subject to minimum 1% commission-in-lieu or exchange. This fee is not applicable for deposits in S$.

## How to apply

- Apply at any of our branches :
  - (for Singaporeans and Singapore PRs) NRIC
  - (for Foreigners) Employment Pass (EP) or S-Pass or Student Pass
  - (for Foreigners) Passport
  - US$1,000 for initial deposit
  - Additional documents : Include any one of the following documents:Phone bill,Half-yearly CPF statement,Any bank statement
