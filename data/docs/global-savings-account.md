## Description

A savings account that allows you tosave in ten major currencies

## Eligibility

Singaporeans, Singapore PRs and foreigners age 18 and above.

## Initial deposit

5,000 units (for AUD, CAD, CNH, EUR, GBP, NZD, CHF,USD; 50,000 units for HKD and 500,000 units for JPY or equivalent in SGD)

## Benefits

- Flexibility to open a savings account in your preferred currency.
- Initial deposit can be done via S$ cheque, deduction from your OCBC account or one-time transfer from another bank.
- The interest rates for foreign currency deposits are updated daily for your information. http://www.ocbc.com/personal-banking/accounts/interest-rates.html

## How to apply

- Apply at any of our branches :
  - (for Singaporeans and Singapore PRs) NRIC
  - (for Foreigners) Employment Pass (EP) or S-Pass or Student Pass
  - (for Foreigners) Passport
  - S$5,000 for initial deposit
  - Additional documents : Include any one of the following documents:Phone bill,Half-yearly CPF statement,Any bank statement
