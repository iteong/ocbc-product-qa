## Description

An account for youth age 16 to 26. It has no initial deposit and minimum balance requirement.

## Eligibility

- You need to be 16 years old and above.
- No minimum income is required.

## Initial deposit

No initial deposit is needed.

## Minimum balance

no minimum balance

## Fees and charges

If you are 26 years old and above, there is a monthly service fee of S$2 if your average daily balance for the month is less than S$1,000.

## Benefits

FRANK ACCOUNT:
- First S$10,000:0.20% per annum
- S$10,001 - S$100,000:0.30% per annum
- S$100,001 - S$500,000:0.40% per annum
- *For balances above S$500,000, there will be no interest given because we believe that your money can work harder with other higher-yielding OCBC Accounts!
Regular Savings Account:
- Starts from 0.05% per annum

## How to apply

For Singaporean/ Singapore PR:
- If Existing OCBC customer:
  - NRIC only
- If not existing OCBC customer:
  - NRIC
  - (Only If mailing in application form)original Singapore phone bill/bank statement/utilities bill within the last 3 months showing resindential address OR Income tax notice of assessment
  - If applying in person at branch, the proof of address is not required
For Foreigner residing in Singapore:
- If Existing OCBC customer:
  - Passport with 6 month validity
  - Valid student/long term/ employment pass
- If not existing OCBC customer:
  - Passport with 6 month validity
  - Valid student/long term/ employment pass
  - Last 3 months documents showing residential address OR Income tax notice of assessment
